# Governance

This repository owns the public portfolio index, onboarding routes, shared vocabulary, and contribution pathways. It does not supersede governance of individual projects.

Current steward: Robert Huffman, creator and portfolio steward. Contributors and reviewers do not automatically become maintainers.

Principles: preserve project boundaries; prefer evidence over claims; expose uncertainty; protect safety and privacy; keep public/private material separate; avoid unnecessary repositories and duplicated specifications; prefer reviewable changes; credit contributions accurately.

Change classes: editorial (clarity/navigation); technical (schema/router/validation); governance (scope/licensing/security); portfolio (project roles or membership). Licensing, governance, and portfolio changes require maintainer approval. Technical changes require tests and review.
